Lead Artist

Remote · Monthly fee, freelance/contractor

Apply

Del Bueno Games is an independent studio from Buenos Aires. We're making Farol Criollo, a co-op city builder set in Latin America, with a stylized semi-realistic look (think Anno 1800 or Manor Lords) and a strong local identity: humble towns, shantytowns ("villas") and everyday Argentine streets.

We're looking for the person who takes charge of the game's art: the art counterpart to our lead programmer.

What you'll own
  • Art direction: define the style with the studio's founder and creative director (palette, level of detail, materials) and keep the game true to it.
  • The art plan: what the game needs, what you make yourself, what we buy and what needs someone else.
  • The team: tell us which other art roles the project really needs, and when. We aim to keep the team small.
  • The budget: you choose the assets and the people, always with cost in mind.
  • Hands-on work: concept art for buildings and street elements, and game-ready models and textures in modular kits.
First tasks
  • The style guide.
  • The art plan: the roles and assets the game needs, and what they cost.
  • Town kit: 3 houses.
  • Shantytown kit: 4 shacks.
  • Then: shops, a warehouse, a police station and street elements.
What we're looking for
  • A portfolio with concept art and finished 3D models.
  • Experience with game-ready models (optimized, with PBR textures).
  • Blender or Maya.
  • Someone who makes decisions and answers for them.
  • Spanish required: it's our working language.
  • Nice to have: Source 2, s&box, Unity or Unreal; architecture or environments; leading other artists.
How we work
  • Remote, as a freelancer or contractor, with a fixed monthly fee.
  • The fee and hours are agreed with you from the first conversation, and dedication can grow as the project does.
  • This is a core role: you'd be one of the people making sure the game gets finished.
How to apply

Send us a link to your portfolio using the form below. In your message, tell us your expected monthly fee and your availability.
